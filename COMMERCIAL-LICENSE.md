# Commercial License Guide (`NetArk`)

`joinblue/NetArk` (`N.E.T.A.R.K.`) adopts a **Dual-Licensing Model** combining **[The Prosperity Public License 3.0.0](LICENSE)** with a **Commercial License**.

---

## 1. Free Noncommercial & Open-Source Use (100% Free Forever)

You do **not** need to purchase a Commercial License if your usage falls under any of the following categories:

- **Personal & Individual Projects**: Personal learning, research, hobbyist coding, and non-monetized personal workflows.
- **Academic & Nonprofit Research**: Use by students, academic researchers, educational institutions, or registered non-profit organizations for noncommercial research.
- **Open-Source Contributions**: Developing and testing free and open-source software (OSI-approved or FSF-approved licenses), or contributing pull requests back to `joinblue/NetArk` under our [Contributor License Agreement (`CLA.md`)](CLA.md).

---

## 2. Commercial 30-Day Trial

Under [The Prosperity Public License 3.0.0](LICENSE), for-profit companies and commercial teams may evaluate `NetArk` for up to **30 calendar days** at no cost (one 30-day evaluation period per legal entity).

---

## 3. When a Commercial License is Required

A paid **Commercial License** is required after the 30-day evaluation period for any commercial or business usage, including:

1. **Enterprise Internal Use (DevSecOps / Agentic Harness)**:
   - Running `NetArk` (including `.netark` extensions, `schemas/schema_validator.py`, `scripts/auto_update_map.py`, or local CLI hooks) on company workstations, internal developer environments, or enterprise CI/CD pipelines to assist in commercial software development.
2. **Commercial Product Integration / Distribution**:
   - Embedding, bundling, or redistributing any part of `NetArk` within proprietary software, commercial SaaS platforms, or paid client deliverables without open-sourcing the resulting product.
